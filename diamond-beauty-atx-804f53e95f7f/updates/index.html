What happens after you save

1. Within an hour (08:00–23:00, Almaty time) we find every place where the old information is used: the knowledge base, texts, captions, videos, the content plan and scheduled posts.

2. The old information is blocked at once — it will not get into any new material. Scheduled posts with the old information are taken off the schedule until we fix them.

3. We fix what can be fixed right away (text, the price on a card, a caption) and put the new version on the review page.

4. Here, under your message, a status will appear: what has been taken into account and what has been fixed. If something is unclear, we will ask right here.

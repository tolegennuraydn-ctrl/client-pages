How to post one as a Trial Reel

  1. On your phone, press Download video under a version below.
  2. Open Instagram → + → Reel → pick the downloaded video → Next.
  3. Paste the caption (button below).
  4. Turn on Trial (on the last screen, above Share). Trial Reels are shown only to people who don't follow you.
  5. Turn on the AI label, then Share. Post the versions a few hours apart, not all at once.

After about a day Instagram shows each trial's views. Tell the LetsChina chat which letter did best — the next videos copy that opening.
